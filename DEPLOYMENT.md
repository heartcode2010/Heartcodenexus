# 🚀 Hosting Heartcode Nexus for Free

Your website is fully configured and ready for free, high-speed global hosting. Here are the 3 easiest ways to host it:

---

## Option 1: Vercel (Recommended — 1-Click Free Hosting)

Vercel provides free ultra-fast CDN hosting, automatic HTTPS/SSL, and automatic updates whenever you push to GitHub.

### Steps:
1. Go to **[vercel.com](https://vercel.com)** and log in with your **GitHub** account.
2. Click **"Add New..."** → **"Project"**.
3. Import your GitHub repository: `heartcode2010/divine-design-mirror`.
4. In the configuration:
   - **Framework Preset**: Other / None (Static HTML)
   - **Root Directory**: `technova-website` (select this folder)
5. Click **"Deploy"**.
6. Within 15 seconds, your site will be live with a free custom URL (e.g. `heartcode-nexus.vercel.app`) and free SSL!

---

## Option 2: Netlify Drop (Instant 10-Second Drag-and-Drop)

If you don't want to connect GitHub right now:
1. Open **[app.netlify.com/drop](https://app.netlify.com/drop)** in your browser.
2. Drag and drop the `technova-website` folder directly into the browser window.
3. Your site is instantly hosted and online with a free `*.netlify.app` address.

---

## Option 3: GitHub Pages (Free via GitHub)

1. Go to your GitHub repository: `https://github.com/heartcode2010/divine-design-mirror`.
2. Click **Settings** → **Pages** (on the left sidebar).
3. Under **Build and deployment**:
   - Source: **Deploy from a branch**
   - Branch: `main` / `/(root)`
4. Click **Save**. GitHub Pages will publish your site in 1–2 minutes.
