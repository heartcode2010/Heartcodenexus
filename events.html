Events & experiences

Make space for learning in public.

Our event formats are designed to make technology more approachable and connect people with ideas, tools and each other.

FORMAT 01

Hands-on workshops

Focused sessions where participants follow along, practise a skill and leave with something they have made.

FORMAT 02

Developer meetups

Community conversations, show-and-tells and peer learning for people building with technology.

FORMAT 03

Hackathons & build days

Collaborative challenges that turn a problem statement into a prototype through teamwork.

FORMAT 04

Career conversations

Practical discussions on portfolios, learning pathways, opportunities and navigating technology careers.

FORMAT 05

School & community outreach

Interactive visits and introductory sessions that help young people discover technology.

FORMAT 06

Online learning sessions

Accessible virtual sessions for demonstrations, Q&A and knowledge sharing across locations.
